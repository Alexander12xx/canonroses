Frequently Asked

Questions from international buyers

What countries do you export to?
We export worldwide to over 10 countries across Europe, Asia, the Middle East, and the Americas, including Netherlands, UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Japan, Russia, and the USA. We can ship to almost any destination with reliable airfreight connections.
What is your minimum order quantity?
Our minimum order quantity is 5 boxes (approximately 1,000 stems) for international shipments. For smaller orders, please contact us and we may be able to accommodate based on your destination and shipping method.
How do you ensure freshness during shipping?
We maintain an unbroken cold chain at 2–4°C from harvest to arrival. Our roses are packed with hydration gels, insulated boxes, and shipped via priority airfreight to ensure they arrive in perfect condition with a vase life of 10–14 days.
Are your roses certified for international export?
Yes, all our roses are fully certified with MPS-A, Fairtrade, GlobalG.A.P., Kenya Flower Council, and ISO 9001. We provide complete phytosanitary certificates and export documentation with every shipment to ensure smooth customs clearance.
Can I request custom packaging or branding?
Absolutely. We offer custom packaging options including branded sleeves, private-label boxes, and custom bunch sizes. Please discuss your requirements with our export team and we will create a solution tailored to your brand.
What is your lead time for bulk orders?
Standard lead time is 5–7 business days from order confirmation to dispatch. For large or custom orders, we recommend placing orders 2–3 weeks in advance to ensure availability and optimal quality.
Do you offer seasonal or limited-edition varieties?
Yes, we offer seasonal varieties and limited-edition novelty blooms throughout the year. Subscribe to our newsletter to receive updates on new and exclusive varieties as they become available.
What payment and shipping terms do you offer?
We offer flexible payment terms including T/T, L/C, and advance payment. Shipping terms are FOB (Nairobi) or CIF to your destination port. We work with trusted logistics partners to ensure reliable and cost-effective delivery.
