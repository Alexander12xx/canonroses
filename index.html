Cultivation & Quality

From equatorial highlands to your cold room.

Every stem passes through five carefully controlled stages before it reaches an international buyer.

  1. 01

    Cultivation

    Grown under controlled greenhouse conditions at 1,900 m altitude.

  2. 02

    Harvesting

    Hand-picked at peak freshness in the cool morning hours.

  3. 03

    Grading & QC

    Sorted by stem length, bloom size, straightness and foliage quality.

  4. 04

    Packing

    Bunched, sleeved and boxed in temperature-controlled packhouses.

  5. 05

    Export & Logistics

    Airfreighted via cold chain to international distribution hubs.
